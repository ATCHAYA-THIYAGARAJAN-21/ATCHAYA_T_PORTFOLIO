Case Study & Scope

Tracking batch expirations is critical in food service and pharmaceuticals. Expiryfy stores manufactured dates, calculates remaining shelf lifespans, and displays color-coded warnings for products nearing their expiration windows.

Key Features & Modules

Interactive Shelf Life Timers

Calculates remaining days using internal date-difference logic, updating countdowns on page load.

Color-Coded Status Warnings

Highlights products in red (expired), yellow (expires in 15 days), and green (safe) to simplify monitoring.

Automated Stock Reports

Generates clean lists of items that need to be cleared or discounted due to upcoming expirations.

Technical Implementation

Developed with standard HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, Expiryfy utilizes responsive Bootstrap 5 styling to present data fields clearly for store operators.
