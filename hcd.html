Case Study & Scope

Protecting personal privacy in changing rooms, hotels, and trial spaces is a growing concern. The Spy Camera Detector is a hardware prototype designed to detect hidden surveillance lenses by scanning for infrared glare and radio frequency (RF) transmissions, warning users via physical buzzers and LEDs.

Key Detection Principles

Infrared Reflection Detection

Projects structured red light from LEDs, highlighting reflection points from curved camera lenses.

RF Wave Frequency Tracker

Scans local frequencies for active wireless transmissions, pinpointing transmitting bugs.

Acoustic & LED Warn Output

Triggers visual signals and buzzers when signal proximity markers are breached.

Technical Implementation

Designed as a portable security device, it uses lightweight electronic circuits and optical filters to isolate lens glare in dark settings, providing a budget-friendly solution for personal privacy.
